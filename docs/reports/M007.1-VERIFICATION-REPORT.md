## 1. Executive Summary

Mission M007.1 eliminates position squatting, unauthorized arbitrary `bytes32 positionId` initialization, origin-to-destination identity ambiguity, and callback identity substitution across CrossGuard's entire three-chain protocol (`Base Sepolia 84532` -> `Reactive Lasna 5318007` -> `Unichain Sepolia 1301`) under strict ₹0 budget, zero-deployment, zero-live-transaction, and non-destructive history constraints.

Key engineering deliverables:
1. **Canonical Domain-Separated Identity Root (`src/libraries/CrossGuardIdentity.sol`):** Standardizes $\text{IDENTITY\_VERSION} = 1$ and $\text{PositionID} = \text{keccak256}(\text{abi.encode}(\text{POSITION\_IDENTITY\_DOMAIN}, \text{originChainId}, \text{originRiskController}, \text{originVault}, \text{originUser}, \text{salt}))$ alongside shared `OriginIdentity` and `RiskCallbackMessage` structs.
2. **Origin Anti-Squatting & Strict Vault Authorization (`CollateralVault.sol`, `RiskController.sol`):** Replaces arbitrary ID deposits/borrows with `CollateralVault.openPosition(salt, asset, amount)` (preserving `_positions` at storage slot `2`), locks `depositCollateral` and `recordBorrow` strictly to `pos.user == msg.sender`, removes owner bypass on `RiskController.assessRisk()` and `registerPositionIdentity()` (`onlyAuthorizedVault` only), binds immutable origin identities with `VaultIdentityMismatch` / `OriginUserMismatch`, and emits versioned 192-byte `RiskBreach`/`RiskResolved` events containing `identityVersion`, `originVault`, and `salt`.
3. **Reactive Canonical Verification (`CrossGuardReactive.sol`):** Adds `originCollateralVault` constructor parameter & getter, rejects legacy 96-byte events (`INVALID_DATA_LENGTH`), dirty upper bits on `version`/`originVault`/`originUser` (`MALFORMED_EVENT_DATA`), unsupported identity versions (`UNSUPPORTED_IDENTITY_VERSION`), unauthorized vaults (`UNAUTHORIZED_ORIGIN_VAULT`), zero salts (`INVALID_SALT`), and `PositionID` preimage mismatches (`POSITION_ID_MISMATCH`), dispatching `RiskCallbackMessage` payloads while preserving all M006 deferral & retry guards.
4. **Destination Identity Binding, EIP-712 / ERC-1271 Delegation & Default-Owner Policy (`CrossGuardCallback.sol`, `BorrowingController.sol`):** Binds immutable `trustedOriginChainId` (`84532`), `trustedOriginRiskController`, and `trustedOriginVault`; removes unauthenticated `registerPosition(bytes32)` and `registerPositionFor(bytes32,address)`; implements same-wallet `registerPosition(bytes32 salt)` and relayer-compatible `registerPositionWithAuthorization(...)` using OpenZeppelin `EIP712("CrossGuardBorrowingController", "1")` + `SignatureChecker` (EOA ECDSA + ERC-1271), monotonic `sigNonces`, and expiry `deadline`. Enforces Default-Owner Policy (`RiskBreach` initializes origin metadata and sets `positionOwners = originUser` only if unregistered, without overwriting delegated owners; `RiskResolved` initializes origin metadata while keeping `positionOwners == address(0)` so resolution never grants ownership).
5. **Fail-Closed Deployment Scripts & Operational Diagnostics:** Updated `DeployOrigin.s.sol`, `DeployReactive.s.sol`, `DeployDestination.s.sol`, `ConfigureReactive.s.sol`, `.env.example`, `script/ops/crossguard_healthcheck.py`, and runbooks to require and verify `BASE_COLLATERAL_VAULT` / `--origin-collateral-vault`, `CrossGuardReactive.originCollateralVault()`, and `BorrowingController.trustedOrigin*` parity fail-closed.
